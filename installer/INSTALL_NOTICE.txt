DUCKTIMIZER — LOCAL INSTALLATION

Free, open-source software under the MIT license. No account, activation key,
purchase or device binding is required.

Administrator privileges are required to change Windows settings. Review each
group before applying it. Debloat removes applications; configuration backups
do not guarantee that removed applications can be reinstalled.

Logs and backups stay on this computer. The application sends no telemetry
and does not contact activation or update services. Updates are installed
manually by the user.
